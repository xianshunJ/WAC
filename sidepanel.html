MAKE IT YOURS

A rhythm that works for you.

Daily limit counts WAC work blocks, and it also marks a day whose assignment estimates exceed it. Busy-time checks cover your primary Google Calendar only. Settings affect new plans and new Google events. An assignment can use its own reminder count instead of this default.

How to connect Google Calendar
  1. Open WAC from the Chrome toolbar and select Connect Google Calendar.
  2. Confirm that Google is using the account whose calendar you want to plan with.
  3. Review Google's permission request and allow WAC to access your calendar.
  4. Return to WAC and find time for your tasks. Review your work blocks, then choose Publish plan to add them to your existing calendar.

WAC checks availability on your primary calendar and adds the work blocks you choose to publish. You can disconnect below at any time.
